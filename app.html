    Help

    Select: Click a node, or use arrow keys to navigate.

    Create: Arrow keys create new nodes from the selected one. Down = child, Left/Right = sibling. If a node already exists in that direction, you navigate to it instead.

    Force create: Ctrl+Shift+Left/Right always creates a new sibling.

    Edit: Press Enter or double-click to edit the selected node. Press Enter to save.

    Delete: Press Delete or click the × button to remove the selected node and its subtree.

    Undo: Ctrl+Z.

    Triangle branch: Click a branch and press the toggle button to make it a triangle.

    Movement arrow: Select a node, then Ctrl+click another node.
